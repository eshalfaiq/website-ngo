Science for Everyone. We are breaking down language and socioeconomic barriers to make STEM education accessible for Pashto-speaking students in rural Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

About Us

Baseerat is a student-founded STEM education and outreach initiative. We recognized that capable students often face barriers due to language, limited educational resources, and a lack of academic guidance.

We work to make scientific knowledge more accessible through Pashto-language physics lectures, tailored educational resources, and direct mentorship. Our mission goes beyond just teaching; we connect students' curiosity with real STEM opportunities.

Recently, one of our mentored students reached the final stage of the NPTC and placed among the top 10 students.
